FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does migration take?

Most teams move a first service in an afternoon. Our CLI imports existing Docker and Terraform configs.

Can we self-host?

Yes. Enterprise plans support a hybrid control plane in your own VPC across AWS, GCP, and Azure.

What happens when we exceed limits?

Nothing breaks. We keep serving traffic and bill overages transparently at published rates.

Do you support monorepos?

Yes — affected-project detection means only changed packages rebuild on each commit.
